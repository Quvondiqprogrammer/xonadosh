Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026 · Operator: XonaDosh (honadosh.uz), Uzbekistan

XonaDosh helps students find housing and roommates. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Account data: username, phone number, display name, password hash
  • User-generated content: housing listings, roommate profiles, photos, contact handles
  • Lifestyle preferences used for matching (sleep schedule, cleanliness, budget, etc.)
  • Device/session tokens for authentication
  • Reports submitted for safety moderation

How we use information

  • Provide listing search, roommate matching, commute and grocery tools
  • Authenticate users and secure sessions
  • Moderate UGC housing/roommate content and process abuse reports
  • Comply with Uzbekistan law and App Store / Google Play requirements

Sharing

Listing and profile fields you publish (name, phone, Telegram, photos, preferences) are visible to other users. We do not sell personal data.

Retention & deletion

You may delete your account in-app. We soft-delete the account, revoke sessions, and remove associated listings and roommate profiles.

Contact

Questions: support via honadosh.uz
